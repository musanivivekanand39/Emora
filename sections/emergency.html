Grounding while you wait

  • Name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear.
  • Breathe in for 4, hold for 4, out for 6. Repeat five times.
  • Move to a room where someone else is, if you can.
  • Put distance between yourself and anything unsafe.
